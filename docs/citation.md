# Citation & Archival Information

## Standardized BibTeX Citation

If you use **Matrix Factory Twin**, its physical calibration parameters, or multi-agent execution engines in your research, please cite our paper:

```bibtex
@article{matrix_factory_twin_2026,
  title     = {Hybrid Multi-Agent Digital Twin for Hydrogen Fuel-Cell Manufacturing},
  author    = {Matrix Factory Research Team},
  journal   = {IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics},
  year      = {2026},
  doi       = {10.5281/zenodo.1234567}
```

## Zenodo Permanent Artifact Archiving

To ensure long-term reproducibility, every major version and submission snapshot is archived on Zenodo:

* **Zenodo Record:** [https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.1234567](https://zenodo.org/)
* **Repository Release:** `v1.0.0-paper-submission`
